About the Author

Ricky Torn is a full-time independent writer specializing in high-stakes contemporary fiction and story-driven narrative sagas. Distributed primarily via Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) and digital storefronts worldwide, Ricky's work focuses on complex character dynamics, fast-paced plotting, and immersive world-building.
